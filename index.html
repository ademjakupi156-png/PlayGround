SURVIVAL NOTES

HOW TO NOT DIE

01

Find the pieces

Your car broke apart on impact. Twenty parts are glinting somewhere in the fog. Walk close to take them.

02

It hears you

Sprinting and torchlight pull it closer. Walking is quiet. Standing still in the dark is quieter.

03

Don't stare

If you see its eyes, keep moving. Shining the torch in its face makes it hesitate — but it will remember.

04

Rebuild and run

When all twenty pieces are found, return to the wreck. The engine still turns. Probably.
